Privacy Policy

Forest Fire Monitor · last updated 20 October 2026

Forest Fire Monitor shows readings and fire alerts from forest sensors. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it).
  • Notification token: an identifier for your device so alerts can be sent to it.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on "Alert me about fires near me" or use directions. Your approximate location is stored so serious alerts within 10 km can reach you; directions use it on your phone only to draw the route.
  • Telegram (optional): if you connect Telegram, your Telegram chat ID and display name, so alerts can be sent there.
  • Your actions: when you acknowledge or resolve an alert, or mark it as a real fire or a false alarm, your name is saved with it.

Why

Only to run the service: sign you in, send you fire and sensor alerts, record who handled an alert, and measure how accurate fire detection is. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications) and processed by a Cloudflare Workers service. Crash reports may be sent to Firebase Crashlytics. If you connect Telegram, alert messages are delivered through Telegram. Maps use Esri, NASA and OpenStreetMap tiles; directions use a routing service that receives the start and end points of the route.

Your choices

  • Turn off nearby alerts or Telegram alerts any time in Settings (or send /stop to the bot).
  • Location permission can be refused or withdrawn in your phone's settings.
  • To delete your account and its data, open an issue at the link below and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/surendar-77/forest-fire-monitor/issues.
